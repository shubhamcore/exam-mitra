How it works — one click, seven agents

1

Parse syllabus

Intelligently splits your input into logical chapters — single topics stay as one, multi-topic syllabi are grouped smartly.

2

Build day plan

Allocates days based on exam weightage and daily hours, with built-in revision + PYQ practice days every 5-6 days.

3

Find resources

Curated free lectures from Physics Wallah, Khan Academy, Vedantu, Mohit Tyagi, NPTEL — with DuckDuckGo + Google Search as backup.

4

Write notes

Revision notes with 8-12 key concepts, 6-15 formulas (with units!), common student mistakes, and an exam-focused summary.

5

Make flashcards

5-8 active-recall cards per chapter mixing formula recall (easy), concept checks (medium), and tricky sign/misconception questions (hard).

6

Generate MCQs

4 exam-style questions per chapter with plausible distractors from real student errors — every answer has a 3-5 sentence explanation.

7

Grade & diagnose

After you practice, an agent grades your answers and pinpoints weak areas with specific chapter-level feedback.
